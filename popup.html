Button guide
Apply / Easy Apply
Open LinkedIn's application action. You complete and submit the application.
Previous / Next
Move between remaining jobs on the current results page. Applied cards are hidden when Hide Applied jobs is on.
Copy description
Full job text, ready to paste.
Copy & mark applied
Copy details and link; confirm an available LinkedIn Yes prompt. Does not submit an application.
Send to ChatGPT
Send immediately to your last used open chat. Existing drafts are preserved.
Save to Sheets
Replace the selected cell in your last used open spreadsheet, then move down one row.
Hide / Restore job
Hide or restore the current job in LinkedIn.
Use the action buttons on the job page.
